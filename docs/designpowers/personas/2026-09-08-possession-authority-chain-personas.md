# Inclusive Personas: Possession Authority Chain

## Meera — Section Controller
**Context:** Coordinates several blocks from a desktop during live traffic.  
**Abilities and conditions:** Situational cognitive overload; may use high zoom.  
**Technology:** Keyboard and pointer, large display, reliable network.  
**Goals:** See conflicts, grant or defer safely, and close a block with a complete record.  
**Frustrations:** Hidden actions, stale status, and colour-only urgency.  
**Environment:** Noisy control office with frequent interruptions.

## Arjun — Field Supervisor
**Context:** Leads work on ballast and records physical acts.  
**Abilities and conditions:** One hand occupied, gloves, glare, intermittent attention.  
**Technology:** Android phone, unreliable network.  
**Goals:** Know whether work may start and record protection once.  
**Frustrations:** Small targets, lost offline work, and ambiguous disabled buttons.  
**Environment:** Bright sunlight, noise, vibration, time pressure.

## Farah — Station Master
**Context:** Endorses T/351 and later closes the station side of the block.  
**Abilities and conditions:** Colour-vision deficiency and second-language English.  
**Technology:** Android phone or control desktop.  
**Goals:** Verify the right form and plan version before signing.  
**Frustrations:** Jargon without references and status conveyed only through colour.  
**Environment:** Multitasking between operational duties.

## Dev — Traction Power Controller
**Context:** Issues and cancels PTW based on current electrical state.  
**Abilities and conditions:** Keyboard-only use after a temporary wrist injury.  
**Technology:** Desktop and the shared Android binary.  
**Goals:** Prevent stale or offline permit assertions.  
**Frustrations:** Incomplete earthing/rod-removal evidence and unclear focus order.  
**Environment:** High-consequence decisions under interruption.

## Kavita — S&T Engineer
**Context:** Records correspondence testing before handback.  
**Abilities and conditions:** Low vision; uses enlarged text and screen magnification.  
**Technology:** Android phone.  
**Goals:** Enter test duration and understand the 30-minute floor before submission.  
**Frustrations:** Clipped text, placeholder-only inputs, and errors announced too late.  
**Environment:** Outdoor work with unstable connectivity.

## Shared inclusive stories
- As an authority using keyboard or screen reader, I can identify the possession, required evidence, action, and outcome without relying on colour.
- As a field worker in glare with one hand occupied, I can use large labeled controls and retain replay-safe observations through a restart.
- As a stressed operator, I can see why an action is unavailable and what must happen next without reconstructing the state machine.
