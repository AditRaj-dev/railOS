# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Control officers and maintenance planners coordinate a live railway corridor from a desktop control room. Field supervisors use the companion view on mobile. Users need to spot risk, understand possession windows, compare candidate plans, and act without losing context.

## Product Purpose

RailOS is a synthetic railway operations control center for planning maintenance blocks, monitoring corridor pressure, coordinating departments, and demonstrating emergency replanning. Success means a user can identify the next safe action quickly and understand why it is recommended.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm under pressure, operationally honest. The interface should feel like trusted railway equipment: legible, procedural, and deliberate rather than flashy or promotional.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI SaaS dashboards, blue-purple neon palettes, glassmorphism, decorative gradients, oversized rounded cards, fabricated marketing metrics, and color-only safety cues.

## Design Principles

1. Operational truth before decoration.
2. Decision hierarchy under pressure.
3. Dense but scannable.
4. Accessible by default.
5. One system across desk and field.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, 4.5:1 text contrast, reduced motion, readable text scaling, semantic controls, and status communication that does not depend on color alone.
