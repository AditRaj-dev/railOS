SEC 01 Design Philosophy

OPERATIONAL FIRST

Railblock is a railway control-room system and maintenance planning console. It prioritizes operational clarity, density without chaos, and decision support over decorative consumer trends.

01. CLARITY
Operational Clarity Over Visual Flair
Prioritize rapid scannability and actionable insights. Every visual element must serve decision-making.
02. DENSITY
Density Without Chaos
Indian Railways operators review thousands of assets. High density is a necessity, organized by strict grid discipline.
03. SURFACES
Dark Surfaces for Control Rooms
Deep calibrated dark theme reduces optical fatigue during 12-hour shifts in railway section control rooms.
04. SEMANTICS
Strong Semantic Status System
Standardized colors for neutral, info, warning, critical, and departments. Never rely on color alone; always pair with text/icons.
05. HIERARCHY
Consistent Hierarchy
Network Situation → Immediate Risk → Today's Operations → Recommended Actions. Avoid 12 equal-weight cards.
06. EXPLANATION
Explain State Changes Clearly
Automated block recommendations and replans must explain their algorithmic trade-offs transparently.

SEC 02 Visual Character & Rules

INDUSTRIAL & PROCEDURAL

The aesthetic identity is procedural, precise, trustworthy, controlled, and low-noise.

Standard Railblock Archetype (Mandatory)
  • Industrial: Resembles real traction and interlocking SCADA consoles.
  • Procedural: Stepwise workflows with clear approval gates.
  • Precise: Tabular numerals, exact timestamps, and section chainages.
  • Trustworthy: Explicit attribution of automated optimizer recommendations.
  • Low-Noise: Subdued neutral boundaries; reserve high-chroma tones for anomalies.
Prohibited Patterns (Anti-Patterns)
  • No excessive gradients or neon glows: Impairs readability under varied display setups.
  • No startup glassmorphism / blurred cards: Adds rendering overhead with zero operational utility.
  • No playful or cartoon illustrations: Rail operations involve safety of life and passenger transit.
  • No oversized rounded bubble cards: Wastes critical screen real-estate.
  • No gratuitous decorative motion: Animations must be under 150ms and convey state transition only.

SEC 15 Mobile Field PWA Specifications

TOUCH & TACTILE CONSTRAINTS

Do not squeeze desktop control-room tables onto mobile. The Field PWA focuses strictly on Today's Assignments, block entry confirmation, delay reporting, and defect logging.

Mobile Design Constraints
  • Minimum Touch Targets: 44px height for field workers wearing heavy gloves.
  • High Sun Contrast: High luminance contrast for outdoor track ballast environments.
  • Offline Resilience: Defect logging must cache locally in IndexedDB when track cuts lack cellular reception.
  • Explicit Confirmation: Starting a block or clearing track protection requires positive multi-step confirmation to prevent safety violations.

SEC 16 Operational Copywriting Rules

ZERO MARKETING JARGON
Approved Operational Terminology
  • “Generate Plan” (Explicit action)
  • “Review Block Recommendation” (Procedure)
  • “Defect at KM 104/12 Reported” (Exact chainage)
  • “Replan Convergence: 4.2s (Optimal)” (Engineering status)
  • “Grant Track Possession” (Railway rulebook terminology)
Prohibited Marketing Copy
  • “Let AI optimize your maintenance workflow seamlessly”
  • “Next-generation smart intelligent maintenance magic”
  • “Effortlessly supercharge mission outcomes”
  • “Delightful rail experiences”
  • “One-click miraculous dispatch”

SEC 17 Accessibility & Synthetic Data Labelling

INTEGRITY & SAFETY
Accessibility Imperatives
  • Contrast: Minimum 4.5:1 ratio on all operational text and badges.
  • No Color Alone: Red critical alerts must include an exclamation icon and text label.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Full arrow-key selection in dense tables and Gantt rows.
  • Screen Scaling: Tested across 1080p control room video walls and 14-inch laptops.
Demo & Synthetic Data Marking

To prevent operational confusion during demonstrations, simulated figures must be watermarked explicitly:

[SIMULATED DATA] [DEMO CORRIDOR] [SYNTHETIC DELAY]
